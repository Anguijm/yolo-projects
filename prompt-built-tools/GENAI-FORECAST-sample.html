6. How it works, and what it is not

  • The model: Level and trend smoothing, a season when asked, a damped trend, weights chosen by a grid on the one-week-ahead error, about a hundred lines of arithmetic.
  • The plan as a known future: Regress the actuals on the plan, smooth the leftover, forecast the line on the future plan plus the leftover's forecast; the idea behind known-future inputs in time-series models.
  • The band: From the model's own past errors by horizon, a normal fallback with few errors.
  • The honesty check: A holdout against "same as last week"; a model that loses is not worth using.
  • What the page is not: A learned model, several series learning from each other, growth the schedule never planned, judgment about why a week ran high.
  • Columns: The real columns are learned by the column-name prompt, never typed into a chat.
