# Taste: the prompt-built work tools

Version 0.1, 2026-10-31, accepted by the owner the same day. Rules the prompts in this folder follow, so a page built inside the work chat model
comes out the way the owner wants it without a second round. Lines marked **(owner)** are his answers of
2026-10-31; lines marked **(derived)** come from his standing rules or from what he has kept and used. The flagship
pages have their own page: `../TASTE.md`.

## Why he reaches for a tool

1. **It opens and works (owner).** One HTML file, no install, no network (the pages run where nothing outside can
   load), system fonts only.
2. **It does what he wants it to do (owner).** The job he came for, finished. A page that loses three of 53 downloads
   is not done, however much else it does (the converter's v0.2).
3. **It shows its numbers (owner).** Every estimate, total and placement can be traced on the page: which history rows,
   which rule, which phase window. A number he cannot check is a number he cannot take to a meeting.
4. **Fast on his real files (derived).** His files are big (a 53-tab workbook, a 1.2 GB tasks export). A tool is
   tested at that size, and it says what it is doing while it works.

## The page

5. **A short how-to on top (owner).** Three or four numbered steps, labels on every control, nothing else.
6. **Built for a work desktop (owner).** A wide screen and a mouse; phone width does not matter for these.
7. **The look is the demo's (derived).** The tokens, cards and color squares of Prompt S, light and dark.
8. **Plain words (derived).** No em dashes, every acronym expanded once, messages that say what happened and what
   to do next.

## When the data is bad

9. **Do the rest and list the misses (owner).** Finish everything that can be finished, then show a plain list of what
   was skipped and why (the sheet, row or job and the reason). Never stop at the first problem, and never guess
   silently.

## How it gets built

10. **His real run is the spec (derived).** Each version answers what he hit at work with the last one. The version
    line says what changed and why, and every version is kept.
11. **Checked against a twin with awkward made-up inputs (derived).** Merged cells, blank and duplicate headers,
    a field named "constructor", line breaks inside quotes, dates as text and as numbers, empty and huge files.
    Nothing from his work records is ever used as a test.
