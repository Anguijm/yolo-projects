# Taste: the flagship pages (Markdown Deck, Naval Scribe, PDF to CSV)

Version 0.1, 2026-10-31, accepted by the owner the same day. Rules a session follows when it builds or changes a flagship, so the owner's taste is
written down before the work instead of discovered after it. Lines marked **(owner)** are his answers of
2026-10-31; lines marked **(derived)** come from his standing rules or from what he has kept and used. The
prompt-built work tools have their own page: `prompt-built-tools/TASTE.md`.

## Why he reaches for a tool

1. **It opens and works (owner).** One file, no install, no sign-in, no network. Opening it is the whole setup.
2. **It does what he wants it to do (owner).** The job he came for, finished, not a near miss with extra features.
   Judge a change by whether it gets that job done, not by how much it adds.
3. **It shows its numbers (owner).** Any count, total or conversion can be traced to where it came from, on the page.

## The page

4. **A short how-to on top (owner).** Three or four numbered steps, labels on every control, nothing else. No help
   section, no feature tour.
5. **It works on his phone (owner).** These three are his own tools, so every one works fully at phone width: no
   sideways scrolling, controls big enough to tap.
6. **Plain words (derived, from his writing rules).** No em dashes, every acronym expanded once, no superlatives.
   Messages say what happened and what to do next.

## When the data is bad

7. **Do the rest and list the misses (owner).** Finish everything that can be finished, then show a plain list of what
   was skipped and why (the row, sheet or page and the reason). Never stop at the first problem, and never guess
   silently.

## How it gets built

8. **A real use beats a spec (derived).** Each version answers something he hit using the last one. The version line
   at the top says what changed and why, and every version is kept.
9. **Checked by someone who did not build it (derived).** A twin or a fresh reviewer recomputes the numbers, with
   made-up awkward inputs (empty files, huge files, odd names), before a version ships.
